How to make a GIF

Record a short clip (5 to 10 seconds) on your phone. Turn it into a GIF with a free converter such as ezgif.com (video to GIF), Canva or Giphy. Keep it short and small (about 480 px wide, 10 frames a second) so it stays under 2 MB. No GIF? Choose 2 to 4 photos instead.
